Inclusive Design

Accessibility Statement (WCAG 2.2 AA)

Effective Date: July 2026

KIBO is dedicated to ensuring digital accessibility for people of all abilities. We continuously audit and optimize our platform against the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA standards.

Accessibility Features Implemented

  • Keyboard Navigation: Logical tab navigation, visible focus indicators, and skip-to-content links.
  • Screen Reader Optimization: Proper ARIA semantics, descriptive alt text, and structured headings.
  • Color Contrast: Minimum 4.5:1 text-to-background contrast ratio across all UI components.

Feedback & Assistance

If you encounter any accessibility barriers on KIBO.IS, please contact our Accessibility Coordinator at: accessibility@kibo.is.
