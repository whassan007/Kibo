Data Processing Addendum (DPA)

Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs), Law 25 terms, and GDPR Article 28 compliance.

This Data Processing Addendum ("DPA") forms an integral part of the KIBO Master Services Agreement between KIBO and Customer. It governs the processing of personal data in accordance with GDPR, CCPA, Law 25, PIPEDA, and HIPAA.

Key DPA Terms Included

  • EU Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs): Module 2 (Controller to Processor) and Module 3 (Processor to Processor).
  • Quebec Law 25 Schedule: Mandatory contractual requirements for cross-border transfers out of Quebec.
  • HIPAA BAA Attachment: Business Associate Agreement terms for healthcare entities processing PHI.
