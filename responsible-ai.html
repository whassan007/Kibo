AI Ethics & Governance

Responsible AI Statement

Guiding principles for KIBO's AI Privacy Copilot and automated agents.

1. Zero Customer Data Training

Customer data is strictly isolated. We do not use customer records or personal data to train public or shared AI models.

2. Human-in-the-Loop Governance

AI models generate drafts, recommendations, and evidence classifications, but external compliance actions always require explicit human approval.

3. EU AI Act Alignment

KIBO provides comprehensive documentation, risk assessments, and audit logs required for compliance with the European Union AI Act.
